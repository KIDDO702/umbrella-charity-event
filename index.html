About the Charity

At Bhai Empire, we believe that the best gift we can give children is joy, which comes from being recognized, celebrated, and loved. Our charity activities are about more than simply donating; they're about bringing people together, sharing smiles, and making memories that will last long after we go.

📍 Venue: Umbrella Children's Home, Kamakis, Ruiru

🕒 Time: From morning to afternoon (exact time TBD)

Donate Items

"Every item donated lets a child know they are important." Their day at the charity event will be brightened by your generosity.

We Accept:

  • Clean clothes (all ages and sizes)
  • Sanitary pads and hygiene products
  • Storybooks and children’s books
  • New or gently used toys
  • School supplies (pens, pencils, notebooks)
  • Non-perishable food items (optional)
  • Shoes and slippers (all sizes)
  • Warm blankets

Donate Time

Spending time demonstrates your concern. In the most significant way, your presence and effort may make a child's day.

Show Your Support:

  • Participate in the charity event
  • Spread the word on social media or with friends
  • Help us organize and set up on the day
  • Remote assistance, every effort matters

Your Trust Matters To Us

Every contribution is handled thoughtfully, responsibly, and openly. Every donation is monitored, and any use is carried out in an open, trustworthy, and shared responsibility manner.
